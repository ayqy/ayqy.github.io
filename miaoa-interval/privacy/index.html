Privacy Policy · Effective August 11, 2026

Timing stays local until you choose otherwise.

MeowIntervals 1.0, displayed on devices as 妙啊节拍, has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or developer account system.

Data stored on your devices

The app saves your interval library, folders, Trash, Countdown presets and current app state in its local containers. Widget favorites use a private App Group on the same device. The paired Watch receives a checksummed projection of supported timers and Countdown presets. This everyday working data is not sent to the developer.

Manual iCloud backup

When you choose Back Up Now, the app places one manual backup of the interval library, folders, Trash, Compound links, and five Countdown presets in its private iCloud container. Active workouts, Stopwatch sessions, active Countdowns, notification requests, music files, and Health history are excluded. Apple operates iCloud under your Apple Account and its own terms.

Health and Music

After you grant permission, MeowIntervals can read the permitted Health values needed for an optional calorie estimate and write workouts that you choose to record. Raw read values are not saved in the app. Music Library access occurs only when you choose a track for workout playback; device-only Music selections are removed from shared timer snapshots.

Store purchases

Apple processes the lifetime Pro purchase, Family Sharing, and restoration. The app receives StoreKit product information and verified entitlement results. MeowIntervals does not operate a payment account or send purchase analytics to a developer service.

When one timer can leave the device

  • .miaoa file or device link: The app creates a privacy-checked copy only when you request it. You choose the destination through the system share interface.
  • Web publishing: When you choose Publish Snapshot, one immutable timer snapshot is sent to the developer-operated publishing service and becomes available through a public link. It may include the timer name, intervals, timing, colors, cues, and other settings needed to import that timer.
  • Excluded data: Published snapshots exclude Health settings and results, Music Library selections, your Apple ID, email, account, folders, and the rest of your interval library.
  • Abuse prevention: Apple App Attest provides an app-installation key and cryptographic proof to authorize publishing. It is not used for advertising, cross-app tracking, or a user profile.

Purpose, linkage, and tracking

The developer collects published timer content only to provide the publishing and import feature. App Store privacy disclosure classifies it as Other User Content used for App Functionality, not linked to your identity, and not used for tracking.

Retention and deletion

A published timer is kept for no more than 90 days and is then automatically deleted. You can withdraw it earlier to block new visits; copies already imported onto another device remain under that recipient’s control. Local data remains until you delete it or the app. A manual iCloud backup remains until replaced, deleted in the app, or removed through iCloud storage management.

Your choices

You choose whether to grant Health or Music access, create or restore an iCloud backup, share a file or device link, publish a web snapshot, or make a StoreKit purchase. System Settings controls permissions. Local timing and file sharing remain available when web publishing is unavailable.

Support messages

A support email leaves your device only when you send it and is processed by the applicable email providers. Please begin with non-sensitive device, system, version, and error details rather than private timer content or credentials.

Questions

Contact privacy support

Please do not include interval names, Health values, Music Library identifiers, App Attest identifiers, publication or revocation tokens, backup files, or other sensitive data in your first message.
