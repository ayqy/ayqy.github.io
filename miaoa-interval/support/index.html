MeowIntervals Support

Keep the timer honest.

Most issues can be narrowed down without sending your interval names, Health details, music selections, or backup files.

Before reinstalling or changing devices

  1. Open My Intervals → Backup & Restore.
  2. Choose Back Up Now and wait for the confirmed backup summary.
  3. Remember that this is one manual iCloud backup, not real-time sync.
  4. After restoring, reconnect Watch and re-authorize Health, Music, alerts, and notifications as needed.

Purchase or restore help

  • Use the same Apple Account that made or received the Family Sharing purchase.
  • Choose Restore Purchases from the unlock screen.
  • Check that the App Store is reachable and purchases are permitted on the device.
  • A pending or unverified transaction cannot unlock Pro until Apple confirms it.

Watch and workout checks

  • Keep iPhone and Apple Watch paired and within connectivity range for library updates.
  • Grant Health access only for the workout data you want to read or save.
  • Background execution, notifications, haptics, Music, and Health can vary with device state and system permission.
  • The last verified Watch snapshot remains available when the phone disconnects.

Sharing and published links

  • A .miaoa file supports larger timers; a device link opens the same import flow.
  • A published link contains one immutable, privacy-checked timer snapshot.
  • Published links expire within 90 days and can be withdrawn earlier.
  • Withdrawing a link does not remove local copies already imported by someone else.

Contact

Email MeowIntervals Support

Include app version and build, iOS or watchOS version, device model, the action immediately before the issue, and the exact message shown. In your first message, do not include interval names, Health values, Music library identifiers, App Attest identifiers, publication tokens, backup files, or sensitive screenshots.

Contact support by email

System scope

MeowIntervals 1.0 (build 1) supports iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later and includes an Apple Watch app. Apple Health, Music, iCloud Drive, notifications, widgets, Family Sharing, and App Attest depend on compatible hardware, system version, permission, Apple Account, network, and regional service availability.
