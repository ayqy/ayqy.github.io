Privacy Policy · Effective August 20, 2026

Local first. Network access when you choose a connected tool.

MeowToolkit 1.0 is maintained by the independent developer ayqy. It has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. Most working data stays on your device.

Data kept on your device

Recents, saved outputs, focus state, app preferences, scan input and results, StoreKit entitlement state, live-data cache, and portable backup files are stored or created locally. A Home Screen widget uses an Apple App Group to read the small amount of app data needed for its display.

Photos and document scanning

The system photo picker gives the app only the items you select. OCR is performed on device. MeowToolkit does not enumerate, edit, or delete your photo library. A PDF or other result leaves the app only when you choose an iOS export or sharing destination.

Network features

  • URL and page extraction: the address you enter is requested to return page details.
  • Live cards: platform share, headlines, fuel prices, and quotes are requested from the source named beside the result.
  • Optional continuity: after you explicitly connect a recovery space and tap upload, recents and saved outputs are sent to the developer-operated continuity service for later restore.
  • App Store purchases: Apple StoreKit provides products, purchase status, restore, and subscription management.

These network providers may receive normal connection information such as an IP address under their own terms. MeowToolkit does not use these requests for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Optional continuity data

A continuity snapshot is classified as Other User Content used only for App Functionality. It is associated with a recovery credential rather than a developer account identity and is not used for tracking. Do not send your recovery code in an initial support email.

Backups, sharing, and support

A local backup or exported result goes only to the destination you choose. Support email is sent only when you compose and send it, and is then processed by the relevant email providers. Avoid including private documents, extracted content, saved output, or credentials unless the developer specifically requests a minimal diagnostic.

Retention and deletion

Local app records remain until you clear them or remove the app; exported files remain wherever you saved them. Clearing local data does not automatically erase an optional remote continuity snapshot. You may request deletion help by email; the developer will first establish a safe way to identify the recovery space without asking for unrelated content.

Your choices

You decide whether to select photos, enter a URL, load a live source, enable notifications, purchase through Apple, create or import a backup, connect continuity, upload a snapshot, share a result, or send support email. iOS Settings controls permissions and notifications.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests

This policy is written and maintained for MeowToolkit by its independent developer, ayqy. In your first message, describe the request without attaching private files or including a recovery code.

Contact privacy support
