Independent iOS app by ayqy

Useful work, without the crowded toolbox.

MeowToolkit brings nine focused utilities to iPhone and iPad: scan selected photos with on-device OCR, extract useful page details, transform developer text, focus with a timer, check clearly named data sources, and keep results ready to revisit.

Scan and extract

Start with the material you choose.

Pick specific photos for on-device OCR and PDF export, or enter a URL to extract page titles, summaries, links, images, and video references. The app does not browse or alter your photo library.

Transform and focus

Small tools with clear jobs.

Format JSON, transform Base64 and URL-encoded text, run a focus timer with ambient sound, and return to recent tools without rebuilding your workflow.

Save and move

Keep useful output reusable.

Saved results and recents remain local by default. You can create a portable backup, merge it on another device, or explicitly upload an optional continuity snapshot.

Source transparency

Live data names where it came from.

Platform share, world headlines, fuel prices, and daily quotes load only when needed from the sources identified in the app. Unavailable data is shown as unavailable or last known—not invented.
