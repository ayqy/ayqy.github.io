Independent iOS app by ayqy

Turn documents into useful results.

Scan, recognize, convert, edit, and export with 37 focused tools. MeowScanner works with items you explicitly capture or select, keeps external originals read-only, and saves new results in its app-managed storage.

View on the App Store

Capture and recognize

From paper to editable information.

Use Smart Scan, ID Scan, table recognition, OCR, and barcode recognition. Camera access begins only when you choose a capture flow; Photos and Files remain behind Apple’s system pickers.

Convert and organize

PDF, images, Word, Excel, and more.

Create new output copies, merge or split PDFs, extract pages and images, and use supported Office conversion paths. Each workflow validates its output before it is saved.

Image utilities

Edit without overwriting the source.

Stitch, compress, watermark, enhance, remove selected regions, and extract tables. The source photo or imported file stays unchanged.

English + 简体中文

Choose the language for this device.

Version 1.1 lets you choose System, English, or Simplified Chinese in Settings & Trust. System uses Simplified Chinese when Chinese is preferred or the device region is China, and English elsewhere. An explicit choice is stored locally.

Local first

Your working documents stay under your control.

No account or subscription is required. MeowScanner has no ads, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-operated document server. Content leaves the app only through an action you choose, such as sharing, exporting, Apple Translation, or downloading from a valid HTTPS address you provide.
