MeowCadence 1.0

Support

Start a complete free 170 SPM, 10-minute run from the main screen. No account or purchase is required.

Hear music or podcasts with the beat

Start your music, podcast, or audiobook first, then open MeowCadence and tap Start Run. The app uses iPhone audio mixing. Output behavior can vary with a Bluetooth route or another app’s audio policy.

Restore Lifetime Unlock

Tap View Lifetime Unlock, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account that made the verified non-consumable purchase. Pending, cancelled, refunded, revoked, or unverified transactions do not grant custom controls.

Background sessions and safety

The production audio path is built for background and lock-screen continuation. If a phone call or another system interruption pauses audio, return to the app and retry. MeowCadence is a recreational timing aid, not medical advice; stay aware of traffic and your surroundings.

Contact support

Email panicmonkey@163.com. Include the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, audio route, action you took, and visible error text. Do not send Apple credentials, transaction identifiers, or sensitive screenshots.
