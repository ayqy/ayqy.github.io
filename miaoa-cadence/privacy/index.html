Privacy

MeowCadence is designed to work offline. The developer does not collect data from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your cadence, duration, session clock, language preference, and locally refreshed purchase entitlement stay on your device. MeowCadence has no developer account system or developer-operated server.

What the app does not access

The app does not request location, Health data, microphone, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or tracking permission. It includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Purchases

Apple processes the Lifetime Unlock purchase, transaction verification, Family Sharing, refunds, and restoration through StoreKit. The developer does not receive payment-card details or operate purchase analytics.

Contact

For privacy questions, email panicmonkey@163.com. Please do not include Apple credentials, transaction identifiers, device identifiers, or sensitive screenshots in your first message.

隐私政策摘要

妙啊步频离线工作，开发者不从 App 收集数据。步频、时长、训练时钟、语言偏好和本机权益状态留在设备上；App 没有开发者账号或自建服务器。

App 不申请位置、健康数据、麦克风、通讯录、照片、广告标识或跟踪权限，也不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK。永久解锁的付款、验证、家庭共享、退款与恢复由 Apple StoreKit 处理。

隐私问题可发送邮件至 panicmonkey@163.com。首封邮件请勿附 Apple 凭据、交易标识、设备标识或敏感截图。
