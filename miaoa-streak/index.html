Make today count.

Plan around real life

Flexible without rewriting history.

Give each habit its own days, weekly target, count, duration, reminders, and steps. Rest days stay neutral, and future changes do not manufacture a better past.

One source of truth

Manual, timed, or connected.

Complete a habit yourself, run a focused timer, or connect a supported Apple system source. History, notes, statistics, widgets, and Shortcuts resolve from the same habit facts.

English + 简体中文

Use the language that fits this device.

Choose System, English, or Simplified Chinese in Manage Habits. System uses Simplified Chinese when Chinese is preferred or the device region is China, and English elsewhere. An explicit choice stays saved on this device.

Optional Apple integrations

Connect only what helps the habit.

Health, Screen Time, Medication, notifications, widgets, Shortcuts, Live Activities, and alarms remain optional and depend on the device, permission, Apple account, iOS version, and service availability.

Local first

Your data moves only when you choose.

  • Keep working data on this iPhone first.
  • Optionally sync portable habit records through private iCloud.
  • Share one selected habit read-only, or create a Full CSV on demand.
  • Keep Health values, Screen Time and Medication selections, permissions, timers, and private system tokens device-specific.
