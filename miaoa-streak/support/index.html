MeowStreak Support

Help without handing over your life.

Most answers are available inside the app under its compact label, Steady. A small, non-private summary is enough to start a support request.

Before switching or reinstalling

  1. Open Manage Habits → Data & Sync.
  2. If iCloud is enabled, resolve conflicts and wait until pending changes reach zero.
  3. Create a recent Full CSV and save it somewhere you control.
  4. After moving, reconnect Health, Screen Time, Medication, alarms, notifications, and any active timer.

Useful report details

  • App version and build number
  • iOS version and iPhone model
  • The action immediately before the issue
  • The exact message shown by the app or iOS

Open Privacy & Support and choose Share Support Summary for a status-only starting point.

Change the app language

  1. Open Manage Habits from the sliders button.
  2. Under Language, choose System, English, or 简体中文.
  3. Your explicit choice is saved locally and remains after the app restarts. System defaults to Simplified Chinese when Chinese is preferred or the device region is China, and English elsewhere.

Contact

Email MeowStreak Support

Describe the problem and expected result. In your first message, do not include habit names, notes, Health values, Screen Time or Medication selections, account or device identifiers, CloudKit IDs, tokens, private files, or sensitive screenshots.

Contact support by email

System scope

MeowStreak 1.1 (build 5) supports iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later. Health, Screen Time, Medication, iCloud, sharing, notifications, widgets, Shortcuts, Live Activities, and alarms depend on device, iOS version, permission, Apple account, and regional service availability.
