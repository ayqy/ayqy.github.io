Privacy Policy · Effective August 9, 2026

Private by default. Transfer by choice.

MeowStreak 1.0, displayed in the app as Steady, has no advertising, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or custom account server.

Data stored on this iPhone

The app saves habits, schedules, targets, completion history, notes, steps, streak policies, preferences, reminders, source connections, permissions, and active timer state locally first. App and extension settings shared on the same device use a private App Group.

Apple system sources

When you explicitly connect a supported source, the app may read the Health metric, workout, mindfulness, daylight, heart-rate, Medication, or Screen Time result needed for that habit. Health values and snapshots, Screen Time and Medication selections, permissions, and private system tokens stay device-specific.

When data can leave the device

  • Private iCloud: If enabled, portable habits, completion history, notes, steps, and streak policies merge through your private CloudKit database.
  • Habit Sharing: When you choose Share, one selected habit is placed in its own read-only iCloud share.
  • Full CSV: A versioned export is created only when you request it, and you choose its destination in the iOS share interface.
  • Support: A support email leaves the device only when you send it and is processed by the applicable email providers.

Excluded from portable transfers

Health values and snapshots, Screen Time and Medication selections, system permissions, active timers, account and device identifiers, CloudKit record identifiers, and private system tokens are excluded from private habit sync payloads, habit shares, Full CSV files, and support summaries.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not create a developer account. Apple provides iCloud, Health, Screen Time, Medication, notifications, widgets, Shortcuts, and related services under Apple’s terms.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete the habit or app. If private iCloud sync is enabled, deletions participate in the record merge. Turning sync off stops new transfers and does not erase an existing iCloud copy. Files, shares, or messages you sent remain under their destination’s control.

Your choices

You choose whether to grant system permissions, enable iCloud, share one habit, or export and import a Full CSV. iOS Settings controls system permissions. Data & Sync shows pending changes and conflicts before you rely on another device.

Questions

Contact privacy support

Please do not include habit names, notes, Health values, Screen Time or Medication selections, identifiers, tokens, or other sensitive data in your first message.
