Use the language that fits this device.

Version 1.1 offers System, English, and Simplified Chinese in Settings. System uses Simplified Chinese when Chinese is preferred or the device region is China, and English elsewhere. An explicit choice is stored locally and restored after relaunch.

Local first

Your records begin on your device.

Everyday and sensitive records are stored in the app container by default. You decide when to export a backup or start optional continuity. Privacy-Safe Backup and Full Backup make their different boundaries explicit before export.

Read the privacy policy

Premium boundaries

Core records stay useful without pretending purchases are backups.

Premium can unlock Full Backup, App Lock, advanced reminders, richer reports, and deeper trend review. Apple handles purchases and Restore Purchases; restoring an entitlement does not restore local record files.
