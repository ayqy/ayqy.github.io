Privacy policy

Your records begin on your device.

MeowPlanner is local first. It has no advertising or cross-app tracking. Record data leaves the device only after an action you choose, such as export, App Store purchase handling, support email, or optional continuity.

Data stored locally

Everyday records, passwords, intimacy records, membership history, App Lock settings, language preference, and backup activity are stored in MeowPlanner’s app container by default. Sensitive modules use local privacy controls. The app does not read, modify, move, or delete unrelated photos, videos, or files on a connected device.

Data that can leave the device

  • Optional continuity: only after you start the action, MeowPlanner sends a privacy-safe subset of non-sensitive records and recovery settings. This content is used for App Functionality, is not linked to an account identity, and is not used for tracking.
  • App Store purchases: Apple handles product loading, purchase, entitlement, and restoration under Apple’s policies. MeowPlanner receives verified entitlement results, not payment-card details.
  • Support: an email leaves the device only when you choose to send it. Do not include passwords, backup files, recovery codes, or private record content in the first message.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

MeowPlanner does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking. Optional continuity content is used only to provide the recovery function you request, not for advertising or user profiling.

Backup, retention, and deletion

  • Files exported through iOS remain at the location you choose and under your control.
  • Deleting a local record removes it from local app state; a later continuity upload can synchronize the corresponding deletion.
  • Deleting MeowPlanner does not remove exported files elsewhere and does not automatically erase an optional continuity copy.
  • For deletion of an optional continuity copy, contact support without sending the recovery code in the first message; the developer will provide safer verification steps.

Your choices

No MeowPlanner account and no optional continuity are required for local use. You control notification permission, backup export, App Lock, language, and whether to start a continuity upload. Export records you want to keep before uninstalling or replacing a device.

Developer and policy updates

This policy is maintained by independent developer ayqy. A material change to MeowPlanner’s data practices will be reflected here with a new effective date.
