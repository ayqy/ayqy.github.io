Keep a backup before you need one.

MeowPlanner stores records on this device by default. Export a backup you control before deleting the app, erasing the device, or moving to another device.

Getting started

  1. Use Quick Capture on Home for a common record, or open Library to choose from all 19 modules.
  2. Add dates and reminders where they are meaningful.
  3. Use Timeline and Review to revisit activity and upcoming follow-ups.
  4. Open Settings → Backup & Transfer before reinstalling or changing devices.

Change the app language

Open Settings and use App Language at the top. System uses Simplified Chinese for a Chinese preferred language or China device region and English elsewhere. English and 简体中文 override System immediately. The selected option remains after you quit and reopen the app.

Backup and transfer

  • Privacy-Safe Backup omits passwords, intimacy records, membership history, App Lock settings, and other device-only security state.
  • Full Backup includes sensitive fields and requires Premium plus a current privacy unlock.
  • Files are saved through the iOS Files interface; MeowPlanner does not move or delete files you already have.
  • Import shows a summary before replacing local data. Keep the original backup until you verify the restored records.

Optional continuity

When the service is available and you start it, MeowPlanner can upload a privacy-safe copy of non-sensitive records for recovery on another device. Passwords, intimacy records, membership state, App Lock settings, and the local service session do not transfer. Keep the recovery code private.

Premium and Restore Purchases

Plans load from the App Store. Restore Purchases asks Apple to recover a current entitlement; it does not restore local records, exported backup files, or a continuity recovery code.

Contact the developer

Include the MeowPlanner version and build, iOS or iPadOS version, selected app language, and the steps before the problem. Do not attach backup files, passwords, recovery codes, or private records in your first email.
