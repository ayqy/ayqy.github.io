Privacy policy

Your records start on your device.

Miaoa is local-first, does not run advertising or cross-app tracking, and only sends record data off the device when you choose an explicit continuity action.

Data stored on your device

Everyday records, passwords, intimacy records, membership history, app-lock settings, language preference, and backup activity are stored in Miaoa's local app container by default. Sensitive modules use the app's local privacy boundary. Miaoa does not read, change, move, or delete photos, videos, or other personal files on a connected physical device.

Data that may leave the device

  • Optional continuity: only after you choose a continuity backup, Miaoa sends a privacy-safe copy of non-sensitive records and settings needed for restore. It is keyed by a service recovery credential rather than a Miaoa account identity.
  • App Store purchases:Apple processes product loading, purchase, entitlement, and restore information under Apple's policies. Miaoa receives verified entitlement results, not your payment card details.
  • Support: information leaves your device only when you decide to email it. Do not send passwords, backup files, recovery codes, or private record content.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Miaoa does not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. Optional continuity data is used for the app functionality you request, not for advertising or profiling.

Backups, retention, and deletion

  • Files exported through iOS Files remain where you save them and are under your control.
  • Deleting a local record removes it from the local app state and a later continuity upload can remove the corresponding remote record.
  • Deleting Miaoa does not delete backup files that you saved elsewhere and does not by itself erase an optional continuity copy.
  • To request deletion of an optional continuity copy, contact support. Do not include the recovery code in the first message; support will provide a safer verification path.

Your choices

You can use Miaoa without an account and without optional continuity. You control notification permission, exported backups, app-lock settings, and whether to start a continuity upload. Before uninstalling or changing devices, export any records you want to keep.

Contact and policy changes

Material changes will be dated on this page. For a privacy question or deletion request, contact Miaoa Support without sending private record content in the first message.
