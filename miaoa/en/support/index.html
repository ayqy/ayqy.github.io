Official Miaoa support

Keep everyday records useful over time.

Start with Quick Capture, revisit what matters in Timeline, and keep a backup before reinstalling Miaoa or moving to another device.

Getting started

  1. Open Home and use Quick Capture to add your first to-do, habit, note, countdown, medication, or wishlist item.
  2. Use Library to reach all 19 record types and manage their details.
  3. Return to Timeline to review dated activity and upcoming follow-ups.
  4. Open Settings → Backup & Transfer before reinstalling the app or changing devices.

Local-first does not mean automatically backed up

Miaoa stores records on this device by default. Export a backup and keep the file somewhere you control before deleting the app, erasing the device, or switching devices.

Backup and transfer

  • A Privacy-Safe Backup omits passwords, intimacy records, membership history, app-lock settings, and other device-only security state.
  • A Full Backup includes sensitive fields and requires Premium plus a current privacy unlock.
  • Backup files are saved through the iOS Files interface. Miaoa does not move or delete files you already have.
  • Import shows a summary before replacing local data. Keep the original backup until you have verified the restored records.

Optional continuity

When continuity is available and you choose to use it, Miaoa can upload a privacy-safe copy of non-sensitive records for recovery on another device. Passwords, intimacy records, membership state, app-lock settings, and the local service session do not transfer. Keep the recovery code private: anyone who has it may be able to restore that continuity copy.

Premium and Restore Purchases

Plans load from the App Store. If the catalog is unavailable, Miaoa keeps the purchase surface honest and lets you try again. Restore Purchases asks the App Store to recover a current entitlement; it does not restore your local records or backup files.

Contact support

Include the Miaoa version and build, iOS or iPadOS version, selected app language, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not attach backup files, passwords, recovery codes, or private record content to the first message.
