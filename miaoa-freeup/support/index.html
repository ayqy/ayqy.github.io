Support

MeowExport batch-exports selected photos and videos to Files on iPhone. The Mac companion is shown as FreeUp.

iPhone selection or export issue

  1. Tap Choose Photos & Videos and select the items again in Apple’s picker.
  2. Keep enough free space for a temporary local copy and the destination copy.
  3. Tap Export to Files, choose an accessible folder, and tap Save. Check Recent Exports for a per-item result.

MeowExport never deletes or edits originals in Photos. If an item is stored only in iCloud, allow Apple’s picker time to make it available before retrying.

Language

Open Settings in MeowExport and choose System, English, or 简体中文. An explicit choice is restored on the next launch.

iPhone not found

  1. Unlock the iPhone and confirm that you trust this Mac.
  2. Reconnect the data cable and close other media apps that may own the device session.
  3. Open FreeUp and use Refresh after the initial discovery attempt finishes.

Permission denied

Review Photos, removable-volume, and Files and Folders access in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then return to FreeUp and retry. Never modify the macOS TCC database.

Export issue

Choose an accessible destination with enough free space. FreeUp does not overwrite existing files; verified files remain available when an export is canceled or partially fails.

Contact

Email panicmonkey@163.com. Include the app version/build, iOS or macOS version, device model, error text, and approximate time. Do not send Apple credentials, device serial numbers, personal media, or full local paths.
