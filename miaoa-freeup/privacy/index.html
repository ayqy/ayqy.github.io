Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026. MeowExport works locally on iPhone and Mac. The Mac app is shown as FreeUp.

Data processed on your devices

On iPhone, MeowExport receives only photos and videos you explicitly select with Apple’s system picker. It creates temporary local copies solely to complete your requested Files export and stores recent per-item receipts locally. On Mac, FreeUp may read media names, types, capture dates, sizes, dimensions, durations, thumbnails, previews, and file contents needed for an export you request. View preferences, authorized export-folder bookmarks, temporary previews, manifests, and receipts remain local.

Collection and tracking

MeowExport does not require an account, contains no ads or tracking, uses no analytics SDK, and does not upload your media, device identifiers, or usage to a developer-operated server. Apple system services process Photos and Files access under Apple’s policies.

Permissions and device changes

On iPhone, Apple’s Photos picker limits access to the items you select, and the Files picker controls the export destination. MeowExport never modifies or deletes Photos originals. On Mac, macOS controls connected-device, removable-volume, photo/video, and user-selected-folder access. FreeUp does not automatically choose a destination or export media; device changes remain disabled by default and require separate verified safeguards if enabled.

Retention and deletion

You can clear iPhone export receipts in the app and remove local app data by uninstalling. On Mac, preferences and caches can be removed through macOS or by uninstalling. Files in a destination you selected remain under your control. Because no developer server stores this data, there is no server-side account-deletion process.
