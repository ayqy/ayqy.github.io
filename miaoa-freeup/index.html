Take your memories with you.

MeowExport is a local-first iPhone and Mac utility for making safe copies of photos and videos. On iPhone, select media with Apple’s picker and export it to a Files location you choose. On Mac, browse and export media from an iPhone you explicitly connect and trust.

Batch export on iPhone

Choose up to 50 photos or videos through the system picker, review the exact queue, then save copies to Files.

Per-item receipts

See a local receipt for each completed, cancelled, or failed export. Originals in Photos remain unchanged.

Organize on Mac

Search, filter, preview, and safely export media from a connected iPhone without overwriting existing files.

No account is required. MeowExport has no ads, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-operated media server. The iPhone app includes System, English, and Simplified Chinese interface choices.

Requires iOS 17 or later, or macOS 15 or later for the Mac workflow. © 2026 MeowExport.
