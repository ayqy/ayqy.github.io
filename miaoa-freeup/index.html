Take your media with you. Free space with confidence.

MeowExport is a native macOS tool, shown as FreeUp on your Mac, for browsing, previewing, and exporting photos and videos from an iPhone you explicitly connect and trust.

Browse clearly

Search, filter, sort, and preview connected-device media in grid or list views.

Export safely

Choose a Mac folder, export an exact snapshot, and receive per-item verification without overwriting existing files.

You stay in control

Device changes are disabled by default. Any enabled deletion path still requires a verified export set and explicit confirmation.

No MeowExport account is required. Normal use has no ads, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-operated media server. The current app interface is in Simplified Chinese.

Requires macOS 15 or later and a compatible iPhone connection. © 2026 MeowExport.
